Changelog

What's new in ClientSniper.

v2.4 — July 2026

  • Launched the Sales Rep Portal, giving affiliate reps their own dashboard for tracking referrals and commissions.
  • Added sales rep application and admin approval flow.
  • Improved lead discovery search relevance for niche business categories.
  • Fixed an issue where saved searches occasionally failed to load filters correctly.
  • Various minor dashboard UI polish and copy fixes.

v2.3 — May 2026

  • Added new outreach email templates for local service businesses (trades, hospitality, healthcare).
  • Improved dashboard load performance, cutting average load time on the leads table significantly.
  • Added CSV export for lead lists.
  • Fixed a bug causing duplicate leads to appear after refining a search filter.

v2.2 — March 2026

  • Introduced website quality scoring to help prioritise leads most likely to need a rebuild.
  • Added bulk actions for tagging and archiving leads.
  • Laid initial groundwork for future CRM integrations.
  • Fixed timezone display inconsistencies in the activity log.

v2.1 — January 2026

  • Reworked the lead discovery search UI for faster filtering by location and industry.
  • Added support for annual billing plans.
  • Improved outreach template editor with variable insertion (business name, city, etc).
  • Fixed several mobile layout issues on the dashboard.

v2.0 — November 2025

  • Major rebuild of the lead discovery engine for broader coverage and faster results.
  • Introduced credit-based plan tiers replacing the old flat-search model.
  • Added account settings redesign, including payment method management.
  • Numerous stability and bug fixes following the migration to the new backend.
