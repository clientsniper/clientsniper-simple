500 leads in 10 minutes. That's not a headline trick — it's genuinely achievable with the right tool. Here's exactly how to do it.

Why Google Maps Is the Best Lead Source

Google Maps has indexed nearly every registered business in the UK. When a business creates a Google My Business profile, Google captures their name, address, phone number, hours, and — critically — whether they have a website. This makes Maps the most comprehensive database of local businesses that exists, and it's free to access.

Google Maps is the only place where "has no website" is a queryable attribute. Every other lead source requires manual research to discover this.

The Manual Method (and Why It's Slow)

You could open Google Maps, search "restaurants in London," click through each result, check if they have a website link, and log your findings in a spreadsheet. For 500 businesses, this takes roughly 8 hours. Per niche. Per city.

The ClientSniper Method

Enter your niche and location. ClientSniper pulls the full list of Google Maps results and runs each one through a website-detection process. Businesses with no website are flagged red. Businesses with an outdated or mobile-unfriendly site are flagged amber. You get a sorted, filtered list in minutes.

The Search Parameters That Work Best

  • Niche: Choose one vertical per search. "Restaurants" returns more consistent results than "food businesses."
  • Location: Start with a specific city or postcode area. Broad searches return more results but less focused leads.
  • Filter by status: "No website" leads are highest priority. "Poor website" leads are second.

What to Do With Your List

Export to CSV. Prioritise by review count (higher = more active business) and rating (4.0+ = quality-conscious owner). These criteria correlate with faster conversions and better-paying clients.
