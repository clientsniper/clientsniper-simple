About ClientSniper

Built by freelancers, for freelancers.

ClientSniper helps freelancers and agencies find local businesses that need better websites — then gives them the outreach tools to reach those businesses before anyone else does. We combine lead discovery with practical outreach templates so you spend less time hunting for prospects and more time closing work.

The Problem We're Solving

Cold outreach is inefficient. Most freelancers and small agencies waste hours manually searching for local businesses, guessing at who might actually need a new website, and sending outreach that goes nowhere because it isn't targeted. We built ClientSniper to fix that — surfacing businesses with genuinely weak or outdated web presences, so every message you send starts from a real opportunity.

Our Story

ClientSniper started in the UK, built by a small team who were freelance web developers and designers themselves. We got tired of spending more time prospecting than building, so we started scripting our own lead lists — and eventually turned that into a proper product other freelancers and agencies could use too.

Our Values

  • Transparency — we're upfront about where our data comes from and how our pricing works, no hidden fine print.
  • No dark patterns — cancelling, downgrading, and requesting refunds should be as easy as signing up.
  • Responsible use of data — we only work with publicly available business information, and we honour opt-out requests promptly.
