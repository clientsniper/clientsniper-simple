FAQ
Common Questions

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes — no minimum term, no fine print. Cancel anytime from your dashboard in one click. Your access continues until the end of the current billing period.

What happens to unused leads?

Unused monthly leads expire at the end of each month. Extra credits you purchase roll over — they stay in your account until you use them.

Where do the leads come from?

Directly from Google Maps — real local businesses with ratings, websites, phone numbers, and often email addresses. All data is fetched live at search time.

How does the student discount work?

Sign up with your university email address or verify via UNiDAYS. The $15/month rate is applied automatically. Student plans are monthly billing only.

How do the 500 free leads work?

Every new account gets 500 leads credited automatically on sign-up — no code needed. They're added on top of your plan's monthly allowance and are yours to use immediately.

Is there a minimum contract?

No contracts, no minimum term. Cancel from your dashboard at any time. Your access runs to the end of the current billing period — then stops. No surprise charges.

How does Enterprise work?

Contact us and we'll build a custom plan for your team — unlimited leads, team seats, custom billing, dedicated support and SLA. Get in touch to start the conversation.
