The 10 Best Niches for Website Clients in 2026 (Data-Backed)

We analysed over 500,000 UK business listings to identify which niches have the highest proportion of businesses with missing or poor websites. Here are the 10 best niches to target in 2026.

The best niche is one with high website-gap rates, reasonable average project values, and business owners who are reachable and motivated to grow.

1. Restaurants & Cafes (68% website gap rate)

The highest volume niche with the highest gap rate. Restaurants benefit enormously from websites — online menus, booking systems, and Google visibility are all proven to increase covers. Budget: £1,200–£2,500.

2. Hair & Beauty Salons (71% gap rate)

Salons live and die by bookings. A website with an integrated booking tool can transform a salon's business. Owners tend to be motivated and understand the value of a polished online presence. Budget: £1,000–£2,000.

3. Tradespeople (Plumbers, Electricians, Builders) (74% gap rate)

The highest gap rate of any professional category. Tradespeople typically rely entirely on word of mouth — meaning a website represents a significant competitive advantage. Budget: £800–£1,800.

4. Dental & Medical Practices (45% gap rate)

Lower gap rate but higher project values. Healthcare practices need websites for credibility, booking, and patient information. Budget: £2,000–£4,000.

5. Gyms & Personal Trainers (63% gap rate)

The fitness industry boomed post-2020 and many independent gyms still lack proper web presence. Class schedules, membership info, and trainer profiles are all easy wins. Budget: £1,200–£2,500.

6. Letting & Estate Agents (52% gap rate)

Independent letting agents often have outdated websites or none at all. Property listings, search functionality, and landlord portals are strong upsell opportunities. Budget: £2,000–£5,000.

7. Cleaning Companies (77% gap rate)

The highest gap rate overall. Cleaning companies are usually small operations that rely on referrals — a website dramatically expands their reach. Budget: £700–£1,400.

8. Auto Garages & MOT Centres (69% gap rate)

Garages are often family-run businesses that haven't invested in digital. Online booking for services and MOTs is a clear, tangible benefit to pitch. Budget: £900–£1,800.

9. Accountants & Bookkeepers (58% gap rate)

Financial services businesses need trust signals above all. A professional website with clear service descriptions and team profiles converts well. Budget: £1,500–£3,000.

10. Independent Retailers (66% gap rate)

Brick-and-mortar shops increasingly need an online presence to survive. Even a simple "shop window" site with opening hours, location, and product highlights helps enormously. Budget: £800–£2,000.
