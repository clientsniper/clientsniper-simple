ClientSniper vs Apollo.io: which is better for finding local business clients?

Updated 2 Nov 2026·6 min read
← Back to Blog

Apollo.io is one of the biggest B2B sales databases in the world. ClientSniper is a much more focused tool: it finds local businesses from Google Maps, pulls their contact details, and lets you email them from the same dashboard. They overlap less than you might think. This page lays out where each one wins so you can pick the right tool, not the most famous one.

Choose ClientSniper if…

You're a freelancer, web designer, marketer or small agency selling to local businesses (trades, restaurants, salons, clinics, gyms) and want leads plus outreach for one flat monthly price.

Choose Apollo if…

You sell to mid-size or enterprise companies and need named contacts by job title, intent data and a sales team on shared seats.

The real difference: who you're selling to

Apollo is excellent when your buyer is "Head of Marketing at a 200-person SaaS company". It is built around people, titles and companies, and it's priced for sales teams.

Most freelancers and small agencies aren't selling to that person. They're selling to the plumber with no website, the restaurant with a 4.7★ rating and a broken booking page, or the dental clinic that never answers its phone. Those businesses live on Google Maps, not in B2B databases. That's exactly where ClientSniper searches.

Search "dentists in Manchester" or "roofers in Miami", filter by rating and reviews, and you have a list of real local businesses with contact details in minutes, ready to email from the same place.

Price for a small agency

Take a two-person agency. On Apollo Basic that's two seats at $49 each on annual billing, so around $98 a month, before any extra credits. On ClientSniper Growth it's $55 a month for 3,000 local leads with outreach and a free sending domain included, and nobody pays per seat.

If you're on your own, ClientSniper Starter at $25 a month is about half the price of a single Apollo Basic seat.

Where Apollo is the better choice

  • You need decision-makers by job title inside larger companies.
  • You want buying-intent signals or a built-in dialer.
  • You run a sales team that needs shared seats, reporting and CRM workflows.

We'd rather you pick the right tool. If that's you, Apollo is a strong product.

Where ClientSniper is the better choice

  • Your clients are local businesses: trades, hospitality, health, beauty, fitness, retail.
  • You want to spot businesses with no website, weak reviews or poor online presence.
  • You want leads, sending and follow-ups in one tool at one flat price.
  • You want to show a prospect a mockup of their new website before the first call.

FAQs

Is ClientSniper cheaper than Apollo?

Usually, yes, for local business prospecting. ClientSniper Starter is $25/month for 1,000 leads with outreach included. Apollo's Basic plan is listed at $49 per user per month billed annually (higher on monthly billing), and it is priced per seat.

Is ClientSniper an Apollo alternative?

For freelancers and agencies selling to local businesses (restaurants, trades, salons, clinics), yes. For finding job-title contacts inside larger B2B companies, Apollo is the stronger tool.

Where does ClientSniper's data come from?

Public business listings, including the Google Maps Platform and public business directories. See our Data Sources page for details and opt-out.

Can I use both?

Yes. Some agencies use Apollo for enterprise prospects and ClientSniper for local businesses, where Apollo's coverage is thinner.
