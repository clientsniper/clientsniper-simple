Objection Handling

"Is it legal?"
Yes. ClientSniper scrapes publicly available business information — exactly the same data you'd find by searching Google yourself. No private data is accessed.

"I already have leads."
Most tools give you the same recycled databases. ClientSniper pulls fresh, hyper-local data directly from Google Maps and Instagram in real time.

"It's too expensive."
One client landed from 500 leads typically covers 6–12 months of the plan cost. The ROI speaks for itself.

"I'll try it later."
New members get 500 bonus leads when they sign up — the longer you wait, the more you leave on the table.
